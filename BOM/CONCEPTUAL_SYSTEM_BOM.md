# IX-StellaratorForge Conceptual System BOM

This legacy category summary is retained for continuity with the IX-Fusion lineage. The authoritative v0.3.0 system-category design inventory is:

- `BOM/SFR1_FULL_SYSTEM_BOM.md`
- `BOM/SFR1_FULL_SYSTEM_BOM.csv`

The full SFR-1 inventory contains **87 unique subsystem rows** and explicitly classifies each row as `DEFINED`, `CANDIDATE`, `SOLVER_DEPENDENT`, or `HARDWARE_DEPENDENT` with its governing evidence gate.

Neither this summary nor the full inventory is a fabrication/procurement BOM. Final quantities, dimensions, currents, pressure ratings, material grades, nuclear compositions/enrichment, tritium inventories, safety setpoints, part numbers, vendors and construction drawings remain gated by high-fidelity physics, engineering review, regulation, licensing and hardware qualification.
