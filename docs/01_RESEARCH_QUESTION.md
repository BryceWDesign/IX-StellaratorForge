# Research Question and Hypothesis

## Primary hypothesis

A six-field-period (C6) non-axisymmetric seed may provide a useful initialization for
stellarator optimization when the objective is driven toward omnigenous/quasi-isodynamic
particle behavior rather than toward visual symmetry.

C6 is treated as an ordinary rotational field-period choice. The project's earlier
"inverted Flower" geometry is provenance for the seed only; it has no mystical or sacred
status in the physics model.

## Null hypothesis

After equal-budget optimization and engineering penalties, the C6 seed provides no
reproducible advantage relative to a matched helical control.

## Current POC question

The release-0.1 proof of concept asks a deliberately smaller question:

> Under the repository's reduced dimensionless field-strength and field-line models, does
> the C6 seed clear all predeclared advantage gates against a matched five-field-period
> helical control?

Current answer: **No.** The committed verdict is `FAIL_OR_INCONCLUSIVE`.

That result does not prove C6 is physically inferior. It means the present lower-fidelity
model does not justify promoting the seed to a stronger scientific claim.
