# Preliminary Patent / IP Landscape

This is a technical search note, **not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion**.

## High-overlap areas found

- **US20240177874A1 — Planar coil stellarator.** Describes encircling coils plus shaping coils that do not encircle the plasma/other coils, including structural field-shaping units. A vNext planar-array branch must therefore be treated as an external engineering reference, not claimed as an IX invention merely because the optimizer rediscovers that architecture.
- **WO2024226565 family — Planar coil stellarator including removable field shaping units.** Relevant to replaceable planar shaping modules and sector maintenance concepts.
- **US20240274304A1 — Stochastic Mantle for Magnetic Fusion Devices.** Relevant to any attempt to claim a globally stochastic edge mantle for spreading heat/particle loads.

## Design response

vNext does not depend on novelty claims. It maintains multiple coil/edge branches and records patent overlap as a separate engineering/legal risk. Scientific merit, patentability, and freedom to operate are different questions and must remain separate.
