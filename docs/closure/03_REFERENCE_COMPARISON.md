# Reference comparison: why the present screens are not automatic kill criteria

The public Helios preconceptual stellarator study provides a useful scale reference rather than a template to copy. It reports an 8 m major radius, 6 T on-axis field, 2.7% volume-averaged beta, ~500 m^3 plasma volume, 958 MW fusion power, H_ISS04=1.4, 1.8 s energy confinement, 20 K HTS coils, a 20 T maximum on-coil field, 1.2 m minimum plasma-coil distance, and an idealized TBR of 1.3.

SFR-1's uniform-pressure screen cannot reproduce profile-peaked reactor studies and therefore must not be used to reject the entire 1 GW class merely because it returns ~705 MW at the initial 3% beta point. Conversely, the existence of Helios does not validate SFR-1. It only demonstrates that similar gross scale targets are not intrinsically nonsensical when high-fidelity profile and equilibrium optimization are included.

The most important contrast is coil authority. The SFR-1 intermediate test deliberately uses only 24 simple encircling filament coils and fails at percent-level B-normal error. Published Helios work instead uses a large programmable shaping-coil population in addition to encircling coils and reports much lower reconstruction error. Therefore SFR-1 must proceed through optimized shaping/single-stage coil design; increasing current in the simple screen is not an acceptable substitute.

Reference: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08027
