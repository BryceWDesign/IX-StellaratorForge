# Safety and Scope

IX-Fusion release 0.1 is a computational evaluation package. It is not a hardware build
package.

The repository intentionally does not provide operational instructions, construction
specifications, dimensions, power levels, fuel-handling procedures, radiation-source
procedures, magnet fabrication instructions, high-voltage RF procedures, cryogenic plant
procedures, tritium-processing instructions, or neutron-shield certification recipes.

A physical fusion experiment can involve hazards including intense electromagnetic fields,
high voltage and RF power, vacuum systems, cryogens, stored magnetic energy, ionizing
radiation, activated materials, hot surfaces, mechanical forces, and—depending on fuel and
conditions—nuclear-material controls. Safe physical work requires qualified institutions,
professional engineering, facility-specific analysis, applicable regulation, and dedicated
safety programs well beyond this repository.

The conceptual system BOM is therefore category-level only. It exists to keep engineering
requirements visible during computational research, not to enable construction.
