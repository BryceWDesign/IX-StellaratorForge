
# Thermal System and Power Cycle

## Screening ledger

The Rev A plant target uses:

- 1,000 MW fusion power;
- 1.15 screening blanket energy multiplier;
- 1,150 MW screening thermal power;
- 40% gross conversion target;
- 460 MW screening gross electric output;
- 120 MW maximum design recirculating-power allocation;
- 340 MW screening net value at that ceiling.

The program requirement is net electric power ≥300 MW after all plant loads.

These values establish a **design budget**, not a predicted power plant.

## Primary heat transport

The reference blanket branch uses helium heat removal. Exact pressure, inlet/outlet temperature, pumping power, channel geometry and pressure drop are uncommitted until blanket materials and thermal-hydraulics are selected.

## Power-cycle competition

Two cycles remain open in Rev A:

- indirect supercritical-CO2 Brayton candidate if material temperatures and intermediate-loop design support it;
- steam Rankine reference if it offers better maturity or integration.

The winning cycle must be selected on whole-plant efficiency, component temperature limits, tritium isolation, maintainability and parasitic power rather than theoretical cycle efficiency alone.

## Recirculating power ledger

G8 must explicitly include at least:

- cryogenic refrigeration;
- magnet power and ramp/protection support;
- ECRH wall-plug power;
- blanket/first-wall coolant pumping;
- vacuum pumping;
- tritium processing;
- pellet/gas fueling;
- plant controls and auxiliaries;
- heat-rejection systems.

Net-electric power cannot be claimed until all of those are included with uncertainty.
