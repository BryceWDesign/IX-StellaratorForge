
# SFR-1 Development Gates

## G0 — Seed and target contract

**Current status: passed at low authority.**

Deliverables: non-privileged seed league, transparent targets, negative controls, parameter-authority ledger.

## G1 — Equilibrium

**Current status: not run.**

Run comparable finite-beta DESC campaigns for 3FP/4FP/6FP plus QA reference and direct-J branch. Promote only converged nested-surface solutions with self-consistent transform/pressure/current profiles. Cross-check finalists with VMEC++.

## G2 — Coils and support

**Current status: not run.**

Generate realizable current systems. Evaluate normal-field error, clearances, peak field, REBCO strain over winding packs, support stress/deflection, quench architecture, manufacturing/alignment uncertainty and maintenance windows.

## G3 — Orbits and neoclassical confinement

**Current status: not run.**

Direct J and guiding-center alpha calculations replace the old bounce proxy. Include collisions and realistic free-boundary fields for promoted candidates.

## G4 — Turbulence and profiles

**Current status: not run.**

Nonlinear gyrokinetic heat and particle flux, particle pinch/fueling compatibility, profile closure and sensitivity.

## G5 — Edge and exhaust

**Current status: not run.**

Select/optimize island or X-point-like topology, model target loads, detachment/radiation, neutral compression/pumping, vessel/blanket integration and robustness.

## G6 — Heating and plasma control

**Current status: not run.**

ECRH ray/full-wave deposition, launch accessibility, startup trajectory, control authority, wall-plug and recirculating power.

## G7 — Blanket, shielding and tritium

**Current status: not run.**

Full 3-D neutronics with realistic ports and materials. Close TBR, nuclear heating, shielding, streaming and damage constraints.

## G8 — Integrated reactor closure

**Current status: not run.**

Couple thermal-hydraulics, tritium inventory, cryogenics, electrical loads, maintenance availability and uncertainty. Demonstrate that the selected core has a defensible positive plant power balance and materially beats matched alternatives on the declared objectives.

## G9 — Hardware

**Current status: not run.**

Progressive component and subsystem validation: conductor/joints, structural prototypes, quench behavior, thermal loops, blanket materials, RF launch, diagnostics, then plasma experiments. Software cannot mark this gate passed on its own.
