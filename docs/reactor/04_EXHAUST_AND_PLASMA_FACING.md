
# Exhaust and Plasma-Facing Architecture

Steady-state fusion power makes exhaust a core-selection issue rather than a downstream component choice.

## Competing divertor topologies

Rev A keeps two primary branches open:

- **island divertor**, especially natural for QI candidates whose edge transform can support a robust island chain;
- **non-resonant X-point-like divertor** if a candidate equilibrium can form and preserve the required topology without unacceptable confinement or coil penalties.

The magnetic configuration and the divertor must therefore be designed together.

## Heat-flux target

The reference engineering target is ≤10 MW/m² steady peak target heat flux after edge radiation, detachment or an equivalent power-spreading mechanism. This is not a current result.

The gate must evaluate:

- target heat flux and spatial peaking;
- neutral compression and pumping conductance;
- detachment accessibility and stability;
- impurity/radiation compatibility with core performance;
- target and coolant thermo-mechanical stress;
- transient/off-normal wall loads;
- erosion/redeposition and lifetime;
- vessel and blanket integration.

A candidate with superb core confinement but no credible exhaust solution is rejected.
