
# Maintenance and Remote Handling

Maintenance is an upstream magnetic-design constraint.

## Rev A requirement

Routine replacement of blanket, first-wall or divertor hardware must **not** require removal of the primary superconducting coil system.

This requirement exists because a reactor that produces excellent plasma physics but traps activated components behind inaccessible coils is not a viable plant architecture.

## Replaceable sectors

Rev A reserves modular in-vessel sectors whose final count, mass and extraction path are geometry dependent. During G2/G5/G7 the optimizer must preserve:

- access windows;
- remote-tool approach paths;
- coolant/electrical/tritium service disconnects;
- alignment features;
- shielding plugs for maintenance penetrations;
- space for component withdrawal without violating primary-coil constraints.

## Maintenance evidence

A promoted design requires a CAD collision study and remote-maintenance sequence showing that the highest-dose replaceable components can actually be removed and replaced. A textual maintenance concept is not sufficient for G8.
