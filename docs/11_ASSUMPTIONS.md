# Assumptions

Release-0.1 assumptions are deliberately visible:

1. Magnetic quantities in the internal model are dimensionless.
2. The reduced field-line ODE is a screening surrogate, not a solved magnetic equilibrium.
3. Rotational transform is parameterized rather than derived self-consistently from coils.
4. The bounce-action variation metric is only a comparative proxy.
5. The C6 and 5-field-period configurations use equal optimizer budgets.
6. Structural metrics derive from a smooth magnetic-axis surrogate, not a coil/support FEA.
7. Blanket, shielding, heat, and cryogenic metrics are normalized penalties without material
   or nuclear certification authority.
8. The RF model is a spatial phased array without full-wave plasma coupling.
9. Monte Carlo distributions represent declared parameter errors, not measured manufacturing
   distributions.
10. No fusion power, alpha heating, tritium breeding, or plant recirculating power is assumed.
