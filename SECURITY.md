# Security Policy

IX-Fusion is evaluation-only computational research software. Do not connect it directly to
real laboratory controls, magnets, RF equipment, cryogenic systems, radiation-producing
hardware, or other operational equipment.

Report software-security issues privately to the repository owner through the contact
channel associated with the repository. Do not include secrets, credentials, export-
controlled material, or sensitive facility information in public issues.

The repository contains no intentional network service and no credential requirement.
External-solver integration is opt-in and local; reviewers are responsible for securing
their own environments and verifying third-party solver packages independently.
