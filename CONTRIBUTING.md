# Contributing

IX-Fusion is source-available under the repository's Research & Evaluation License, not an
open-source project. Public source-code derivatives are not authorized by default.

Technical critique, falsification results, bug reports, literature corrections, and
independent benchmark observations are welcome through the repository's review channels.
A useful report identifies the release, configuration, exact command, environment, and
machine-readable evidence artifact involved.

Scientific claims are accepted only when they can be reproduced from versioned inputs.
A proposed change that makes a result look better but weakens a control, removes a failed
run, relaxes a gate after seeing results, or obscures a limitation will not be treated as an
improvement.
